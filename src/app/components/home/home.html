Welcome to the Pokémon Gym Leader Database

Explore the Gym Leaders of the Kanto and Johto regions. Learn about their locations, ages, Pokémon teams, gym badges, and their famous descriptions and mottos.

Kanto Region

Kanto is the first major region introduced in the Pokémon world. It is home to many iconic locations including Pallet Town, Pewter City, Cerulean City, and Viridian City.

The region features eight Gym Leaders who challenge Pokémon Trainers before they can advance to the Pokémon League.

Johto Region

Johto is the region introduced after Kanto and is known for its traditional towns, ancient history, and connection to legendary Pokémon.

Johto also contains eight Gym Leaders, each specializing in different Pokémon types and located throughout the region.
